## Core Content / Detailed Guide

### Online Payments User Guide for Insight.com Invoices

#### Overview:
This guide provides instructions on how to make online payments using either an existing Insight account or as a guest, offering flexibility and convenience when paying invoices through the platform.

---

1. **Initiate Payment**: Click the "Pay Now" button from your invoice.
2. **Select Invoices**: From your list of open invoices, choose which ones you wish to pay; multiple payments can be made at once if needed.
3. **Adjust Payment Amount (if needed)**: If a partial payment is required for an invoice, adjust the dollar amount and select the "Short Pay" option.
4. **Add or Select Payment Method**:
   - First-time users making their first payment must add banking information by clicking “Add,” entering bank account details including routing and account numbers, agreeing to terms, and saving the method.
5. **Review Information**: Verify total amount due, selected bank accounts (for Insight Account Users), and confirm that all relevant data is accurate before proceeding with finalizing your transaction.

6. **Important Processing Times Note**: Payments may take 2-3 business days to reflect in one’s account; if a payment was made on Monday, it might be uploaded by the Tuesday following receipt of funds into Insight's system.
7. **Benefits Using an Insight Account**:
   - Convenience and Efficiency: The process uses single sign-on for users who are already logged-in which streamlines future payments and enhances security through saved payment methods.
  
For those without a specific account at Insight.com:

8. **Access the Payment Link**: Click "Pay Now" to reach the payment page where you’ll need to enter your account number, zip code, complete CAPTCHA verification or other required verifications.

9. **Enter Account Information**:
   - Input your unique account and zip codes associated with this specific Insight.com account.
10. **Final Steps as a Guest**: After entering the above information and completing any necessary verifications (CAPTCHA), proceed by selecting invoices to pay, adding payment methods if required (since it cannot be saved for guest users) – similar to an Insights Account holder but ensuring bank details are entered fresh with each transaction.

---

#### Key Reminders:
- If an Insight employee logs in as the client using the "Login As" feature, the Pay Now button will become disabled.
- Access to Pay Now is granted by user permission and can be controlled via roles and permissions on their account settings.
- Payment options are limited to debit or ACH; credit card payment requires approval from Insight personnel. 
- Guests cannot set up recurring payments, whereas those using single sign-on (SSO) will have the ability to save their accounts’ bank information for future transactions.

### Q&A (For Fine-Tuning)
1. 
    - Question: How do I set up recurring payments?
        - Answer: Recurring payment setups are currently not available to guest users who pay via this method.

2.
   - Question: What should be done if the system takes longer than expected for a payment to appear in an account after processing it online?  
       - Answer: Payments may take 2-3 business days to reflect into your account; ensure all bank details are accurate, and review transaction confirmations for any discrepancies.

3.
   - Question: How secure is making payments using this platform?
        - Answer: The system supports single sign-on which saves user payment methods securely. It also has a CAPTCHA verification process in place on the guest checkout page to protect from bots and unauthorized access.
