## Core Content / Detailed Guide

### Payment Methods for Insight.com Invoices

#### Method 1: Account Payment (For Users with an Insight.com Account)

**Steps to Pay Using Your Insight.com Account**

1. **Initiate Payment**: Click the "Pay Now" button on your invoice, which will take you directly through a single sign-on process.
2. **Select Invoices for Payment**: Choose from open invoices and select those you wish to pay in one transaction or multiple ones together.
3. **Adjust Payment Amount (if needed)**: If paying a partial amount is required, adjust the dollar amount on your invoice before confirming payment details.
4. **Add a Payment Method**:
   - The first time making this payment, enter banking information by clicking "Add" and providing routing and account numbers to agree with terms conditions.
5. **Select Payment Method**: Pick which bank or payment method you would like to use from the options available after adding your banking details for future transactions.
6. **Review Confirmation Details**: Before completing the transaction, verify all amounts, selected bank accounts, and confirmation dates by reviewing on a final page before submission.

#### Important Notes:
- Processing times are 2-3 business days as payments may not reflect immediately in an account.
- Convenience with single sign-on process for managed payment methods is available via your Insight.com login account only. 

#### Method 2: Guest Payment (For Users Without an Insight.com Account)

**Steps to Pay Using the "Pay As a Guest" Feature**

1. **Access and Click**: On receiving an invoice, click on the provided link that leads you directly to payment information entry.
2. **Enter Account Information**: Provide your account number and zip code associated with this payment.
3. **Verification Step (CAPTCHA)**: Complete CAPTCHA or similar verification steps ensuring you are not a robot.

**Additional Considerations for Guests**

- Guest payments do not offer the ability to save bank accounts, preventing recurring or managed payment setups.
- Unlike SSO users who can manage their banking and account info directly within Insight.com's system.

### Q&A

1. **Q**: What payment methods are supported for account payments?
    **A**: Supported methods include ACH, debit transfers, credit cards (approval only), and bank accounts.
   
2. **Q**: How long does it take to see a reflected payment in my Insight.com account after making an online payment?
    **A**: Payments may not be immediately visible; they usually reflect within 2-3 business days.

3. **Q**: Can I set up recurring payments using the guest checkout option on Insight.com's platform?
    **A**: No, guests cannot establish or manage scheduled payments as their accounts do not save payment methods for future transactions.
   
4. **Q**: What should I expect in terms of system features if I pay with my own account versus paying as a guest?
    **A**: Paying through your Insight.com account allows you to add and save bank details, whereas the guest checkout requires entering new information every time.

5. **Q**: How does CAPTCHA or verification affect the payment process for non-account holders on Insight.com's platform?
    **A**: Completing these security checks is necessary before verifying transaction details; they are part of ensuring you're not a bot and adding safety measures to online payments.
