## Core Content / Detailed Guide

### Online Payments Methods - Insight.com

#### Overview:
This guide covers two convenient ways to make online payments for your invoices with Insight.com: logging into an existing account or paying as a guest without one.

#### How To Pay:

**1. Using Your Existing Account**

- **Step 1**: Click the "Pay Now" button from your invoice.
  
- **Step 2**: Select which open invoices you wish to pay, allowing for multiple payments at once.

- **Step 3**: Adjust payment amounts if needed (e.g., partial payments), then proceed with adding a payment method. The first time this is done, you need to enter bank account details:

    - Click "Add" button.
    - Enter the necessary banking information including routing and account numbers.
  
- **Step 4**: Select your desired payment method from those saved.

- **Step 5**: Review all entered data on a confirmation page before finalizing. The system will process payments within two to three business days after submission, as per Insight's processing times noted in the transcript.

**2. Paying As Guest**

- Access "Pay Now" link.
  
- Enter your account number and zip code associated with it.
  
- Complete CAPTCHA or other verification steps to ensure you're not a robot before continuing through similar selection and payment method steps as outlined for users logging into their Insight accounts, albeit without saving the entered information.

#### Important Notes:

1. The Pay Now button is only available if an Insight employee does **not** log in on behalf of the client.
2. Access to this function is user-based rather than account-based; it’s granted upon login authentication.
3. Payment methods accepted are either debit or ACH, with credit card payment options requiring approval.

---

### Key Features / Specifications:
- Convenience and efficiency for users logging into their Insight accounts vs additional steps required when paying as a guest without an account.
- Single sign-on capability reduces the need to enter banking information again on subsequent payments from registered accounts.
- Payment history tracking allows reviewing previously paid amounts.
- Open credits can be applied towards future invoices.

### Q&A

**Q**: Where can I find more details about setting up recurring payments for online invoicing through my Insight Account?

**A**: Recurring payments are not available when paying as a guest; they require an existing account that allows single sign-on and payment method storage.
